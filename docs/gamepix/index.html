A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

One touch. Many changes.

Every tile is either dim or glowing. Your job is to make every tile glow at once.

✺

Ordinary tiles flip themselves and adjacent tiles above, below, left and right. No wraparound at the edges.

◇

Echo tiles add the four diagonal neighbors. Echo power is tied to the tile you touch.

↶

Each move can be undone. Restart is free. A hint highlights a step that belongs to a valid solution from your current board.

Try tap or click. For keyboard play, focus a tile, move with arrow keys and press Enter or Space. Press R to restart, H for a hint, and Escape to close this guide.
